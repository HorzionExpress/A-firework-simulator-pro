Firework Simulator Pro‌ is a cozy browser-based firework sandbox built with vanilla JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas. Want to light up the night sky without actually burning down your backyard? This is the spot. 🎆✨

How to play
‌
Click anywhere‌ on the canvas to launch fireworks directly from your cursor
‌
Browse the side panel‌ — pick from handheld sparklers, ground fountains, sky rockets, cake displays, professional shells, commercial shows, and even drone shows
‌
Switch scenes‌ between city skyline, harbor night, and mountain village vibes
‌
Toggle Business Mode‌ if you wanna grind — buy fireworks, earn virtual cash, and unlock the really expensive stuff

Hit milestones, collect badges, and watch your fireworks empire grow. Or don't. No pressure 😎

What's inside
‌
7 categories & 30+ firework types‌ — from ¥2 sparklers to ¥50,000 drone dragon shows
‌
3 beautifully lit scenes‌ with unique skylines and atmospheres
‌
Realistic physics & particle effects‌ — each firework has its own explosion pattern, color palette, and trail behavior
‌
Procedural sound design‌ — boom, crackle, whistle, all generated with Web Audio API (no audio files!)
‌
Business mode with economy‌ — balance your budget, watch your net worth climb
‌
Milestones & achievements‌ — 20+ badges to collect along the way
‌
Live stats tracking‌ — total spent, earnings, fireworks launched, all the numbers
‌
Local save‌ — your fireworks empire persists between sessions
‌

Mute toggle‌ — for when your roommate complains about explosions at 2 AM
